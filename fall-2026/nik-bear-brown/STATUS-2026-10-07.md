# Where the job-search project stands — 2026-10-07

## Executive summary

**What this is.** A status report on Professor Bear's job-search project: the Lectern collector, the Reallocation Engine it draws on, the three class folders that carry it, and the public work meant to close the gaps the search found. Written on 2026-10-07 at his request, from the files, with every claim pointing at where it lives (Appendix A).

**Where it is.** The tool is built and has run once. Lectern reads 18 company job boards across three applicant-tracking systems, pulled 3,446 postings on 2026-09-26, and kept 97 that match the target: jobs that teach, advocate, or make teaching materials for a named audience. A companion report ranks the companies by evidence that they want this work done, with Anthropic at the top on all five kinds of evidence. Five Tier-1 companies (Google, Adobe, Salesforce, GitHub, Shopify) cannot be read by the tool and are watched by hand or not at all. The three class copies of the shared code were brought back into sync and pushed today.

**Is it running automatically?** No. Every run has been by hand, and there has been exactly one: 2026-09-26, run three times that day as the filter was tightened. Nothing on this machine schedules it, by design and by record: the design document marks a daily run as out of scope, and the only launchd agents on the machine are the film loops. A daily run is a decision still to make (next steps).

**The target, in one line.** Advocate, educator, and materials-making roles at AI and design-tool companies, on flexible terms where they exist; the recipe's own finding is that those terms are rarely posted, so the route is "network, don't apply," and the gap-closing work is public, not private.

**The gaps and what has been done about them.** The CV never mentioned Figma, design systems, certification programs, or conference talks. Since 2026-09-26: a 36-film YouTube series, *Figma for Educational AI*, built and published in its own playlist; a certification rubric drafted from those films' experiments; a second book, *Brutalist Figma + Claude*; and an existing thread with Figma's CEO and Education team that the series is the follow-up to. The book chapters and workshops behind the series are not written yet.

**Next steps** are in the section of that name: decide on scheduling, read the reject sample, decide on the five unreadable companies, finish Branding's Assignment 3 deliverables (due 2026-10-02, overdue), and the human reviews that are pending on Bear.

## 1. What has been built

### The collector: Lectern
One standard-library Python program, `lectern/collect.py`, plus three audit scripts. Master copy in the Computational Skepticism instructor folder; byte-identical copies in Branding (`assignment-3/`) and Prompt Engineering (`lectern/`), kept in step by `lectern/sync.sh`.

| Script | What it does | How it's run |
|---|---|---|
| `collect.py` | Fetch every posting from the watch list, normalise, dedupe, validate, filter by `keywords.json`, write `all-jobs-<date>` and `jobs-of-interest-<date>` (JSON and CSV) plus the raw API responses. Three hosts allow-listed, HTTPS only, no redirects. | `python3 collect.py` (by hand) |
| `audit_rejects.py` | A random sample of rejected postings plus the closest calls, with a blank Verdict column for a human to fill. | by hand |
| `audit_titles.py` | Classifies every title into one of 20 job-function families and reports false positives and candidate misses. | by hand |
| `demand_report.py` | Ranks companies, not postings, on five kinds of evidence that they pay for this work: BUILDS, TEACHES, ADVOCATES, SELLS, ENABLES. Location and employment type are context, never a filter. | by hand |
| `sync.sh` | Copies the shared files to the other two classes; `--check` reports drift. First real run was today. | by hand |

### The configuration, which is the real product
- `sources.json`: the watch list (18 boards, 5 unreachable companies, the reason each is watched).
- `keywords.json` v0.4.0: the filter, with every word that was added, measured, cut, or rejected, and why.
- `title_families.json` v0.3.0: 20 title families (4 keep, 4 judge, 12 reject).
- `ATS.md`: which applicant-tracking system each company uses, and an honest "unknown" for ten.
- `facts/professor-bear-cv.json`: the CV as structured facts, attested 2026-09-23; two sections added 2026-09-27 are not yet reviewed.

### Earlier iterations, kept
- `figma/`: the first single-company pass (Figma's board, 160 postings, 2026-09-23) with `find_roles.py` and `pick_postings.py`.
- `greenhouse-watch-demo/`: the Reallocation Engine's board watcher run on Figma with the CV; it found a scoring-report bug that was fixed in the engine.

### Skills
One skill exists for this project: **greenhouse-watch**, in the Reallocation Engine repo (`.claude/skills/greenhouse-watch/`). It watches one board (Greenhouse, Ashby, or SmartRecruiters), diffs it against the last run, matches new postings against a JSON résumé by documented rules, and stops at a human gate. It has a published deep-explainer film ("greenhouse-watch — Your Board, Your Call", 3:08). No skill wraps Lectern itself; Lectern is a script with a watch list and a filter, run by hand.

### The Reallocation Engine
The repo behind the course assignments. Five recipes are runnable (gate-behavior, gate-harness, local-wage-adjustment, skill-demand-monitor on samples; workday-connector live); the rest are drafts. `scripts/ats/` has Greenhouse, Lever, and Ashby providers. A case recipe for this search, `recipes/cases/2026fa/nikbearbrown-advocate-education-search.md`, is drafted and not pushed. Two assignments were written for it on 2026-09-23 and remain uncommitted in that repo.

## 3. What job roles are being looked at

The filter keeps a posting if its **title** carries a role word, or its **body** carries three topic words, or its body says the job is to **make teaching materials for a named audience**.

- **Role words (45):** advocate, developer/designer/creator advocate, developer relations, devrel, evangelist, educator, instructor, trainer, teaching, education, developer education, product education, curriculum, courseware, instructional design(er), learning designer, learning experience(s), content engineer, technical writer, documentation, docs, academy, certification, enablement, community, workshop, and their variants.
- **Topic words (12):** university, campus, student, classroom, k-12, higher ed, curriculum, educate, teach, and variants.
- **Materials rule:** one of 23 verb-object phrases ("build curriculum", "write tutorials", "record walkthroughs", "on-camera"…) plus one of 29 audience phrases ("for developers", "for educators", "learners", "new hires"…).
- **Flexible terms** (part-time, contract, summer, hourly…) are recorded on a kept posting but never cause a keep.

What it rejects by family: machine-learning "training" (the named false friend), technical enablement, recruiting, sales, customer support, marketing, design, research, product, engineering, G&A, operations. Words cut after measurement: "developer experience" (10 of 11 hits were backend engineering), "advocacy", bare "learning" (42 titles, almost all ML), "content strategy", and eleven materials phrases that matched support, legal, and IT postings.

**The 97 kept on 2026-09-26, by company:** Anthropic 25, OpenAI 21, Stripe 12, Canva 10, Figma 9, Notion 5, Supabase 4, GitLab 3, Replit 3, and one each at HubSpot, Vercel, Webflow, Netlify, Writer. Headline finds: Anthropic's Technical Documentation and Content Engineer (Claude Docs), Replit's Learning Experiences Creator, Stripe's Training Program Manager, HubSpot's Academy Professor, Webflow's Senior Developer Educator (the only US-remote education role found anywhere). Remote advocacy roles: GitLab Senior Developer Advocate, Supabase DevRel Engineer (three), Webflow, HubSpot.

**Measured quality:** title audit, 23 false positives and 1 candidate miss among the kept, 555 postings left in "judge" families; the body rule went from 101 kept at one-in-ten precision to 5 kept with 4 right.
