# Churn 2026-10-18: are these jobs turning over, or sitting there?

## Executive summary

**What this is.** What the saved board snapshots say about postings that stay up versus postings that turn over, for 17 readable boards and 3,243 open postings on 2026-10-18. Snapshots only: it cannot see hires.

**Age.** 29% of today's open postings were first published more than 90 days ago, 14% more than 180 days ago.

**Flow, 2026-09-26 to 2026-10-08 (12 days; 5 of 6 boards trusted).** 1,767 open at the start; **234 disappeared** (13%) and **232 appeared**; 4 of the new ones look like a re-post of one that disappeared. Left out as untrusted reads: Canva (197 to 126).

**Flow, 2026-10-08 to 2026-10-18 (10 days; 6 of 6 boards trusted).** 1,891 open at the start; **182 disappeared** (10%) and **160 appeared**; 6 of the new ones look like a re-post of one that disappeared.

**Flow, 2026-09-26 to 2026-10-18 (22 days; 15 of 17 boards trusted).** 3,089 open at the start; **698 disappeared** (23%) and **742 appeared**; 14 of the new ones look like a re-post of one that disappeared. Left out as untrusted reads: Canva (197 to 110), Webflow (27 to 0).

**Same job under a new post id, 2026-09-26 to 2026-10-18 (9 Greenhouse boards that carry the job id).** 1,871 distinct jobs behind 1,943 posts at the start; **441 jobs disappeared** and **505 opened**; **24** new post ids belong to a job that was already open (a re-post, a new location or a second post for the same job).

**What it cannot say.** A posting that disappears was filled, cancelled, merged or re-posted under a new ID. The board does not say which, so none of these numbers is a hiring rate. A board's posted date can reset when a company edits or re-posts, which makes postings look younger than they are.

## How to read the columns

- **Read trusted?** The monitor's own rule: a board whose open count moved by more than 30% either way between two runs is more likely a read problem (an empty or partial fetch) than that many closings, so it is shown but left out of the totals. A real freeze or layoff would be excluded too; the table is where to look.
- **(date looks reset)** Every posting on that board is under 30 days old, so its posted date is probably an updated-at date and its ages say little.
- **Implied mean days open** is (average open postings) divided by (postings that disappeared per day). It is a fair estimate only if the board is roughly steady (its open count moved by 10% or less); rows that are not steady are marked.
- **Median age of the disappeared** is how old they were when last seen: a floor on how long they stayed, because they may have gone any time before the next snapshot.
- **Re-post-like** counts new postings with the same company, title, place and department as one that disappeared. It will miss a re-post whose title changed and will catch a genuinely identical new opening. It is a lead, not a verdict.
- **New post, same job id / Jobs that disappeared** (Greenhouse boards only): Greenhouse gives every job its own id apart from the id of each post. A new post id whose job id was already open is the same job posted again or posted in another place; a job that disappeared lost all its posts. Postings for a job are counted once in the job columns, so these are fewer than the post counts.
- **Same title open under several IDs** is the same title, place and department open more than once today. Some are genuine multiple openings (many identical sales roles); some are duplicates.
