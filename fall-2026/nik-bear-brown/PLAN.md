Written 2026-10-18 from the Canvas export and this folder's own log.

## Executive summary

**What this is.** One numbered folder for each of the twelve assignments in this section's Canvas course, five published and seven not, each with a `PLAN.md` that says what the brief requires, what already exists, what is still to do, and a step-by-step plan to a rough draft. This file is the map across all twelve. The briefs themselves are published in [`assignments/fall-2026/`](../../assignments/fall-2026/) with the same numbers and names.

**Why it matters.** Professor Bear does the assignments the way students do and logs them the same way. Until now only Week 2 (the Boondoggle Report) and a stray Assignment 3 folder existed here, and three of the five published assignments had work scattered across other folders and repositories.

**What was found.**
- **One is due.** The Pitch Film (05) is due **Sun Oct 25, 11:59 PM ET**. The film exists (3:12, native 4K, every automated gate passes), Professor Bear watched it, and it was published unlisted on 2026-10-18 in the "Professor Bear Does His Assignments" playlist. The reject sample it depends on is still unread; the submission folder is not assembled.
- **Three published assignments are past due and unfinished as examples:** Week 1 (02) has the films but not the paperwork; Week 2 (03) has two competing reports on different applications; Mode Design (04) has a drafted recipe but no mode file in the brief's format.
- **Seven are unpublished** (06 to 12), carry summer 2026 due dates, and have no work done as those assignments. Related material exists for several (see their plans). Whether any is used this term is Professor Bear's decision.
- **Assignment 01** is a student comment task with nothing for the instructor to produce.
- **What cannot be repaired:** Week 1's prediction written *before* the work was never recorded. It must not be backdated.

**Next move.** Decide the questions under "Decisions, all in one place". Then work the order below.

## Order of attack to rough drafts

Rough draft means a complete first pass that Professor Bear can read and refine, not a finished piece.

1. **Now to Oct 25: the Pitch Film (05).** It is the only deadline. The film is out; read the reject sample, decide whether the film is rebuilt, and assemble the folder. The reject sample is the long pole and is the human part.
2. **In parallel, free of Professor Bear's time: 04 mode file, 02 source list and disclosure draft, 03 gap list.** Claude Code can draft all three; each stops at a point that needs his words (the domain justification, the last disclosure field, the own-voice paragraph).
3. **After 05: finish 03, package 02, finish 04.** These three are past due for students and have the most work already behind them.
4. **Then decide 06 to 12.** 08 (the 25-point Mode Build) is the likeliest to be used because it matches the 25-point weekly size and extends 04. 09, 10, 11 and 12 are large and would each need their own plan decision first.

## How this folder is organised

- Folder names are `NN-name-in-kebab-case`, matching the brief's file in `assignments/fall-2026/`.
- Each folder's `PLAN.md` is the working document. Existing work moved in rather than being copied: `boondoggle-report/` became `03-week-2-boondoggle-report/` on 2026-10-18.
- Shared Lectern code stays where it is (`lectern/`, `facts/`, `figma/`, `greenhouse-watch-demo/`). Numbered folders never receive shared files, and `sync.sh` does not touch them.

- Whether any of 06 to 12 will be published. They are shown here because they exist in the Canvas course.
- Whether the Reallocation Engine's canonical repo has the file layouts the briefs describe; the working copy used for this plan calls the equivalent artifacts "recipes".
