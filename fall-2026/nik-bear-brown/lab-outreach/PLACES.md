# Places to read for the lab outreach search

"Think tank" is used here in Professor Bear's sense (2026-10-19): a group of very smart people working on hard problems. An institute, a center, a lab or a policy shop all count, so the Broad and the Whitehead are think tanks. The kind of place is not a filter; the topic is.

Listed 2026-10-19 from Professor Bear's own list and a round of web searches. Institutions only, no people. A place is "confirmed" only where a search result in this session said so; the rest are named by him or listed as not yet verified. Whether a place has lab pages that can be read, and what its robots file allows, has not been checked for any of them except the Harvard Medical School Division of Nutrition.

## Executive summary

**What this is.** The institutions inside Professor Bear's range (mostly remote, on site about once a week, within about one to three miles of Northeastern, Mass General at the far end, not Tufts), grouped by area, with how sure each entry is.

**What was found.**
- **Longwood is the cluster.** Harvard Medical School, the Harvard Chan School of Public Health, Harvard's dental school, the pharmacy school (MCPHS), and the hospitals and institutes around them. One search confirmed Brigham and Women's, Beth Israel Deaconess, Boston Children's, Dana-Farber and Joslin as members of the Longwood Medical Area, and MCPHS's campus at 179 Longwood Avenue, across from Harvard Medical School.
- **The Broad and Whitehead are in Cambridge, not Longwood.** The Broad is a genomics and bioinformatics institute in Cambridge that partners with MIT, Harvard and the five Harvard teaching hospitals.
- **Business-side places are in Cambridge and Allston.** MIT Sloan's Initiative on the Digital Economy (with a GenAI Lab since spring 2025), Harvard's Berkman Klein Center in Cambridge (fellows are expected in residence there), the HBS AI Institute (formerly the Digital Data Design Institute) with its Laboratory for Innovation Science, and the Kempner Institute for natural and artificial intelligence at 150 Western Avenue in Allston.
- **Filtered by his five interests (biology, finance, economics, the MIT Media Lab, people doing interesting things with education).** A research snapshot he pasted on 2026-10-19 listed about 50 Boston and Cambridge institutions and centers; 26 fit at least one interest and are in the table under "Filtered by interest" below, the rest are named there so he can overrule. Biology and economics are well covered; **finance is thin** (the Boston Fed and public-finance shops, nothing on markets), and **education is thin** (the snapshot has almost nothing on it).
- **A finding about money.** The same snapshot says federal research cuts since 2025 hit Massachusetts science institutes hard (its sources: a Boston Globe survey in which 68% of nearly 4,000 NIH-funded scientists said their work had shrunk, and more than $1.3 billion in terminated NIH grants by September 2025, some later restored). I did not check this. If true, the target rule that a lab needs funding to pay for him bites harder on the science side than on the economics and business side.
- **Still open:** which "think tanks" near the Broad he first meant. The snapshot's answer is the Kendall Square institutes (Whitehead, Koch, McGovern, Picower, Ragon); the economics and AI centers (MIT Initiative on the Digital Economy, MIT FutureTech) are in Cambridge too.
